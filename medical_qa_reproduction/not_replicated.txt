Departures from the paper and what was not rerun
=============================================

1. We do not round each condition mean before subtracting them for Table 3.
   We subtract the full-precision means, then round only the final display.
   This avoids differences caused by premature rounding.

2. We do not compare different review populations in the language analysis.
   Both language styles use the same 363 reviews and the two shared question
   types: Effectiveness and Efficacy. The paper's Figure 5 uses 368 technical
   reviews but only 363 plain-language reviews.

3. We do not mix all 16 technical templates with just two plain-language
   templates when estimating the framing-by-language interaction. We use the
   same two question types in both styles. The paper's reported interaction
   appears to use the mismatched template populations.

4. We do not label a framing coefficient pooled across language styles as a
   plain-language-only result. The plain-language effect is fitted separately.

5. We do not treat repeated observations from a review as independent when
   calculating uncertainty. Standard errors, confidence intervals and p-values
   are clustered by review. The paper reports ordinary unclustered errors.
   This changes uncertainty estimates, not fitted logistic coefficients.

6. We do not reuse the authors' saved cosine/entity scores or citation/number
   lists. compute_metrics.py calculates these from the generated answer text.
   The published Table 3 is only a comparison target; it does not supply values
   to our calculations. We do not tune extraction rules to force a match.

7. The paper does not fully specify answer-section extraction, the spaCy model,
   citation/number regexes, entity normalization, or empty-set handling. We make
   these choices explicit at the top of reproduce.py rather than claiming to
   have recovered the authors' undocumented implementation. Current choices:
   Full Answer where present; en_core_web_sm; lowercase entities; bracketed
   article IDs; numbers excluding citations; zero overlap for two empty sets.
   The alternative empty-overlap convention is included in the sensitivity CSV.

8. We do not regenerate the LLM responses or evidence-direction judgments.
   The assignment explicitly permits reusing both. We do not rebuild the
   Cochrane dataset or retrain the pretrained PubMedBERT and spaCy models.

9. We do not reproduce the separate RQ2 analyses/figures, which are outside
   this assignment. Table 3 still includes the four conversation-turn templates
   alongside the twelve single-turn templates, as in the original table scope.

Data handling
-------------
The 48 required JSON input files are copied into this project's data_pre/ folder.
The original files under code/outputs/ are retained and unchanged. Both Python
scripts point at the copies. Intermediate response metrics belong in data_post/.
Final tables, figures and regression results belong in outputs/. All tunable
model and analysis settings are at the beginning of reproduce.py.

The directory reorganization did not rerun calculations. The stopped independent
metric run has partial results for six models under data_post/partial_metrics/.
Existing plots/tables have not yet been refreshed for the new settings.
